How Modern LLM Chats handle context:

What you think happens:
  Turn 1: [Your message] → Model answers
  Turn 2: [Your message] → Model remembers Turn 1

What actually happens every single time:
  Turn 1: [System prompt + Your message] → Model answers
  Turn 2: [System prompt + Turn 1 Q + Turn 1 A + Your message] → Model answers
  Turn 3: [System prompt + Turn 1 + Turn 2 + Your message] → Model answers

The entire conversation history is re-sent on every turn. The model is stateless — it has no memory between calls. 
The "memory" lives in your application, not the model.
